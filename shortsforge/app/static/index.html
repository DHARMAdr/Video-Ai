        Settings
        
  
        
    
        
      
        Assistant
        
      
        The assistant needs an AI to think with. Pick one. ShortsForge sends your chat to that AI. Nothing else leaves your PC unless you switch on web search below.
        
      
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
          
          
          
          
        
        
        
        
        
        Download once, then it works without internet. It runs on this PC and your chats stay here. It is much less capable than Claude. Close games before using it.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        To edit videos by chat, pick a model that supports tools (for example llama3.1 or qwen2.5 in Ollama).
        
      
        
      
      
      
        Without a key, the built-in search asks DuckDuckGo, Bing, Mojeek, Wikipedia and others, and reads the best pages. With a free key from tavily.com it asks Tavily first. Your search words are sent to these services.
        
      
      
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Video folders
        
      
        Videos, Desktop, Downloads, Documents and Pictures are searched already. Add other folders (one per line), for example your recording drive, then press Save above.
        
      
    
        

    
        
      
        Troubleshoot
        
      
        If something fails, make a report and paste it into a chat with Claude. It holds no passwords or keys.
